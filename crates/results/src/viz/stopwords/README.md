Stop words for anchor text clouds (spec 022 plan D6), one word per line.

`en`, `de`, `fr`, `es`, `it`, `pt`, `nl` and `ru` are the Snowball stop word
lists (https://snowballstem.org/), comments removed. Their licence (BSD 3-Clause) is in `LICENSE-snowball`.

`he` is our own short list of Hebrew function words.
